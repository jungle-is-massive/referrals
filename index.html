1
Think of 2 people
Start with people you know, not cold prospects. Past clients, past collaborators, or people who’ve recently moved.
2
Copy, tweak, send
Use the message bank. Lift the copy, make it sound like you, and send it through LinkedIn or email.
3
Log what happened
Track whether they replied, whether a meeting was booked, and keep the momentum visible across the business.
4
Get rewarded
When your intro turns into business, you get paid: £50 for an intro, £250 for a qualified lead, £500 on a signed deal over £20k, and £1,000 over £100k. Rewards are paid through payroll.
